Forward-Deployed Engineering

My name is Shiva Preetham Chinthalacheruvu. As a Forward Deployed Data Engineer currently working on enterprise telecom data pipelines at Frontier/Verizon, I operate directly at the intersection of technical architecture and business operational outcomes.

Rather than building pipelines in isolation, I embed with stakeholders to translate legacy business logic (often buried in legacy Informatica workflows or stored procedures) into modern, scalable PySpark and Databricks solutions. In my current role, this direct collaboration resulted in a 42% reduction in pipeline runtimes, the decommissioning of costly legacy infrastructure, and an automated reconciliation engine diffing billions of records across on-prem EDW and cloud object stores.
