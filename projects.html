SECTION 01 · AI END-TO-END

ANTeater — Open-Source Grocery Scanner

Daily consumer utility · Barcode to health score in a second · Runs entirely in the browser
Live

Why I built it

I grocery shop every day and relied on Yuka to check what's in my food, which meant paying a subscription for a lookup anyone could do with open data. ANTeater replaces it: scan a barcode and get a transparent 0–100 score, the additives driving it (with sources), and a higher-scoring product from the same category.

  • Transparent score: nutrition 60 (Nutri-Score), additives 30, organic 10; any high-risk additive caps it at 49. Unit tested, method documented with sources.
  • Better options: searches Open Food Facts for higher-scoring products in the same category and region.
  • AI where it helps: for products not in the database, Gemini reads a photo of the ingredient list into the same scoring engine, and can explain any score in plain English.
  • Spend tracking: log prices, see monthly spend and how much went to Poor or Bad items, export CSV/JSON for analysis.
  • Resilient demo: installable PWA with an offline shell, on-device caching, and graceful handling of API rate limits (HTTP 429/503).
How a scan flows
1. Capture
Camera barcode decoding with the browser's BarcodeDetector, falling back to ZXing on iPhone.
2. Lookup
Open Food Facts API (no key) with an on-device cache and saved snapshots as a fallback.
3. Score
Pure-function scoring engine and additive risk reference, covered by tests in CI.
4. AI (optional)
Gemini structured-JSON output for label OCR and explanations; auto-selects the newest Flash model.
Fork it freely. No keys to steal.

There is no backend and no stored credential. Product data needs no key, and the AI features are Bring-Your-Own-Key: each person pastes their own free Gemini key, which stays in their browser and goes only to Google. Every push is secret-scanned, and main only changes through checked pull requests.
