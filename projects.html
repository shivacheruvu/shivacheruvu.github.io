SECTION 01 · AI END-TO-END

SmartGrocery AI — Open-Source Yuka Replacement

Daily Consumer Utility · Multimodal Vision OCR · Cost-Optimization Engine
Active Casing Deployed

The Mission: Solving Everyday Grocery Overhead with Open AI

I grocery shop every day and rely heavily on food quality scanning apps like Yuka to avoid toxic additives. However, existing commercial apps lock ingredient insights behind recurring paywalls, sell user habits, and do nothing to help shoppers optimize grocery bills.

SmartGrocery AI is an end-to-end open architecture replacing Yuka while introducing real-time local price arbitrage. Shoppers snap a barcode or nutrition panel; the system runs multimodal OCR through Gemini Vision, evaluates additives against toxicology research, and cross-references nearby grocery stores to calculate cost savings on healthier alternatives.

Replicate On Your Own — Zero Resource Leakage Defense

To protect my cloud infrastructure from outside billing exploitation, this app implements a strict client-side Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) model. Visitors can fork and run this system using their own free Gemini API key without consuming my quotas or accessing my accounts.

Micro-Tool 01 14-Day Trial Guard
Databricks Trial Rapid Bootstrap & Auto-Shutdown Daemon

Automated YAML and Python daemon that provisions single-node Databricks clusters with an immutable 15-minute inactivity termination rule. Sets up Unity Catalog schemas (bronze, silver, gold) and halts all idle compute to eliminate surprise overages.

cluster-config.yaml — Cost-Capped
cluster_name: "trial-cost-capped-cluster" spark_version: "14.3.x-scala2.12" node_type_id: "Standard_D4ds_v5" autotermination_minutes: 15 # Strict trial limit spark_conf: "spark.databricks.cluster.profile": "singleNode" "spark.master": "local[*]" custom_tags: "Environment": "14DayTrial" "CostCenter": "ZeroOverage"
Micro-Tool 02 GCP Free Credit Safe
GCP Free-Tier Lakehouse Starter & Billing Caps

Terraform and Bash scripts to configure Google Cloud Storage lifecycle rules (auto-transition to Nearline/Coldline after 30 days) and BigQuery query-byte quotas so queries can never exceed free GCP trial allocations. Configures keyless GitHub Actions via Workload Identity.
